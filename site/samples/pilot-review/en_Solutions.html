6. In triangle ABC, the constructed line uAv is parallel to BC. The marked angles at A on that line are p and q; the triangle angles are α, β, γ. Use alternate interior and straight angles to prove α+β+γ=180°. Then, with BC extended to D, prove the exterior angle E at C equals α+β.

(4)
α+β+γ=?
Because uAv ∥ BC, p=β and q=γ by alternate interior angles. Along the straight line at A, p+α+q=180°. Substitute to obtain α+β+γ=180°. Also E+γ=180° along BCD, so E=α+β. This is a deductive proof, not a measurement of this particular drawing.
