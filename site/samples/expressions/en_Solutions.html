4. Suggest one possible next term. Explain a rule that fits all four given terms. Is that continuation forced by the four terms alone?

(3)
6,8,10,12,?
One possible next term is 14, continuing consecutive even numbers. A different clearly explained rule fitting every given term may give a different continuation. The prefix alone does not force a unique next term.

Local sample marking: All 3 marks require manual review. Manual review: the rule must fit all four given terms and the proposed next term. Acknowledge non-uniqueness. A different consistent rule is not wrong merely because it differs from the model. No algebraic recurrence is required.
