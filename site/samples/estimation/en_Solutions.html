8. Optional enrichment (JS-3-4), entirely manually reviewed. A trip is approximately 14 km, with an expected moving speed around 20–30 km/h. There may also be stops. Propose a useful estimate for the decision, choose your own justified precision/strategy, state assumptions and check reasonableness. Give an alternative or range and a limitation. Put a short proposal in the answer and details in the reason. Several responses can be valid.

(4)
14;20–30
Using 25 km/h gives about 14/25 h moving time. Using 20–30 km/h gives roughly 7/15–7/10 h before stops. Route uncertainty and stops can change the total; add and justify any allowance rather than calling it exact. Accept other useful justified precision choices.

Local sample marking: All 4 marks require manual review. Review the decision, assumptions, strategy, precision, plausible alternatives/range and limitations. Evaluate the whole proposal manually; the worked example is not a unique answer.
