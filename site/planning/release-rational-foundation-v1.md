# Rational and irrational numbers — foundation candidate v0.14.0

Development candidate, 23 November 2026 (Hong Kong). This branch adds RI01–RI04 and RI-L1/RI-L2 in English and Traditional Chinese. It contains 60 curriculum families and 29 bilingual author-draft lessons across nine topics. It has not been deployed to the live site. All 237 curriculum records remain planned.

The foundation route covers integer nth roots (indices 2–5), principal square-root notation and signs, exact rational/irrational classification, and strict integer bounds for positive/negative nonsquare roots. Classification includes zero, integers, fractions, terminating/explicit recurring decimals, perfect roots, nonsquare roots, pi and an exact finite approximation to pi. Native MathML renders radicals and recurring notation. Reference diagrams do not mark a requested answer. Every explanation and sketch remains manually reviewed.
