## Update rule

At each content release, record the retrieval date and exact source version. If EDB finalises modelling revisions, compare the final documents with this overlay, record deltas and applicable school years, then version the affected inquiry records. Preserve old cohort profiles. Recheck HKEAA when implementing exam-style templates; curriculum alignment alone does not certify an examination profile.

## Attribution and extraction limits

Curriculum wording, classifications and source remarks are attributed to EDB/CDC. This package is for educational curriculum planning. Original PDFs govern mathematical symbols and layout. The ledger normalises line breaks and common glyphs; it does not claim independent translation. Source remarks are preserved in both languages alongside site-authored boundary summaries. A scope note does not substitute for reading the linked source section during task authoring.
