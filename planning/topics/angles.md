# Angles and parallel lines / 角和平行線

22 November 2026 · P2.1 preparatory design · not an implemented lesson

Purpose: Use stated geometric facts to justify conclusions rather than trusting the appearance of a sketch.

根據已知幾何條件推論，並為結論提供理由。

Scope: JS19.1–19.5: basic angles including complementary/supplementary; transversals/pairs; parallel tests and properties; triangle interior/exterior deductions and reasons.

Boundary: An angle-pair name does not require parallel lines. Equality properties do. Keep the given premise distinct from the desired conclusion; no unmarked parallel/right/equal assumptions. Triangle proof needs manual evidence.

Preparation: Degree measure, straight/round/right angles; identify lines, vertices and rays; simple subtraction.

## Three-session learning path

| Session | Opening and purpose | Closing takeaway | Separate check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GE-L1 — Name the relation before calculating / 先辨關係，再求角度 | Compare a vertical pair with a straight-line adjacent pair; identify pairs on nonparallel lines. | Local sums/equalities and angle-pair positions have different roles. | Find an angle with a written reason and classify a fresh rotated angle pair. |
| GE-L2 — Given parallel or proving parallel? / 已知平行，還是要證平行？ | Put givens and desired conclusion side by side for a property and a test. | Known parallelism permits angle deductions; suitable angle data can establish parallelism. | Include valid, near-miss and insufficient-information claims; retain the stated angle relationships. |
| GE-L3 — From a diagram to a deduction / 由圖形建立推論 | Draw a parallel through a triangle vertex and account for a straight angle. | Triangle-sum and exterior-angle rules follow from prior facts; each step needs a reason. | Compute a fresh interior/exterior angle and write a proof outline; no numeric score certifies proof. |



Full session narration is P2.2 work. Each session will use overview → readiness → explanation/visual → model → fading guidance → fresh check → takeaway/next link. Titles and family samples below are bilingual drafts; full lesson prose is not yet authored.

## Six distinct family briefs

These IDs are local family identities. Number changes are variants, not additional families. All examples are newly authored.

## GE01 — Use basic angle relationships / 運用基本角的關係

**Mapped objectives:** JS-19-1 (all Foundation in the retained ledger).

**Demand:** Identify the relevant local angle relation before calculating an unknown.

**Parameters and guards:** Modes: straight-line adjacent sum 180°, point sum 360°, vertical equality, complementary sum 90°, supplementary sum 180°. Positive whole-degree component angles; all shown sectors nondegenerate; do not split these value modes into extra families.

**Answer contract:** Exact degree value in a field with ° printed outside; reason/model rubric separate.

**Engine readiness:** Numeric and reason handling exists; extend geometry model beyond the synthetic adjacent-angle fixture.

**Visual and text alternative:** Ray vectors determine angles; label the vertex, lines and equal/right/straight structure. Text names the relation as a non-visual alternative without relying on drawn scale.

**Sample prompt (EN):** Two adjacent angles lie on a straight line. One is 48°. Find the other angle x and give a reason.

**例題（繁體中文）：** 直線上的兩個鄰角，一個為 48°。求另一角 x，並說明理由。

**Model response:** 132

**Solution (EN):** x = 180° − 48° = 132°, because adjacent angles on a straight line sum to 180°.

**解說：** 直線上的鄰角和為 180°，所以 x = 180° − 48° = 132°。

**Hint (EN):** What is the total angle on a straight line?

**提示：** 一平角共有多少度？

**Counterexamples and feedback checks:**

- Reject 48° unless the task specifically gives a vertically opposite/equal relation.
- A correct number alone does not establish a correct reason.

**Paper target:** 2 marks, 4 working lines, about 3 minutes. Calculator not required (local pilot choice). Automatic result marks and manually judged reasoning/method marks must be displayed separately.

**Review state:** design draft; sample self-check only; no generator, independent review, classroom evidence or published resource is claimed.

## GE02 — Identify pairs without assuming parallel lines / 不假設平行而辨認角對

**Mapped objectives:** JS-19-2 (all Foundation in the retained ledger).

**Demand:** Recognise a transversal and corresponding/alternate-interior/same-side-interior pairs from position, even for nonparallel lines.

**Parameters and guards:** Two distinct intersections of a transversal with two lines. Include explicitly nonparallel cases; preserve angle-pair identities under rotation/reflection and stable labels. Do not infer equality from pair names.

**Answer contract:** Stable-ID classification choice; no numerical equality claimed without givens.

**Engine readiness:** Choice marker exists; a ray/intersection diagram model and label collision checks are new work.

**Visual and text alternative:** At both intersections mark the chosen sectors using labelled rays. Accessible text specifies intersection, side of transversal and inside/outside band; do not describe only colours.

**Sample prompt (EN):** A transversal t meets lines l and m at different points. The two marked angles are in the same relative position, one at each intersection. Name the pair; l and m are not assumed parallel.

**例題（繁體中文）：** 截線 t 分別與直線 l、m 相交於不同點。兩個標示角各位於一個交點，且相對位置相同。指出它們是哪類角對；題目沒有假設 l、m 平行。

**Model response:** corresponding

**Solution (EN):** They are corresponding angles. This describes their positions; it does not by itself assert equal sizes.

**解說：** 它們是同位角。這名稱描述相對位置，本身並不表示角度一定相等。

**Hint (EN):** Classify positions first; check parallelism only when using an equality property.

**提示：** 先按位置分類；使用相等性質時才檢查是否已知平行。

**Counterexamples and feedback checks:**

- Include a nonparallel drawing with this same classification.
- Do not accept an equality claim merely because a pair is corresponding.

**Paper target:** 1 marks, 3 working lines, about 2 minutes. Calculator not required (local pilot choice). Automatic result marks and manually judged reasoning/method marks must be displayed separately.

**Review state:** design draft; sample self-check only; no generator, independent review, classroom evidence or published resource is claimed.

## GE03 — Deduce angles from given parallel lines / 由已知平行線推算角度

**Mapped objectives:** JS-19-4, JS-19-2 (all Foundation in the retained ledger).

**Demand:** Use a given parallel premise and an identified angle pair to deduce a size with a reason.

**Parameters and guards:** Parallel pair explicitly stated and marked; transversal crosses at distinct points; acute crossing angle 20…80°. Modes corresponding/alternate equality or same-side supplementary, plus a bounded two-step local-angle variant.

**Answer contract:** Exact degree number plus manual reason linked to named parallel lines and transversal.

**Engine readiness:** Numeric/reason handling exists; new shared parallel-line geometry model is required.

**Visual and text alternative:** Parallel arrows are model-derived, angle labels/arcs and degree values computed from one crossing parameter; a text description states the same givens.

**Sample prompt (EN):** Lines l and m are parallel. A transversal forms corresponding angles of 68° and x. Find x and give a reason.

**例題（繁體中文）：** 已知 l ∥ m。一條截線所形成的一對同位角分別為 68° 和 x。求 x，並說明理由。

**Model response:** 68

**Solution (EN):** x = 68°, because corresponding angles formed by parallel lines are equal.

**解說：** 因為平行線的同位角相等，所以 x = 68°。

**Hint (EN):** Use both the named angle pair and the given parallel lines.

**提示：** 同時利用角對的名稱和已知的平行條件。

**Counterexamples and feedback checks:**

- Reject 112° for this corresponding pair; it would be supplementary rather than equal.
- When parallelism is absent, disable this property path instead of guessing from the drawing.

**Paper target:** 2 marks, 4 working lines, about 3 minutes. Calculator not required (local pilot choice). Automatic result marks and manually judged reasoning/method marks must be displayed separately.

**Review state:** design draft; sample self-check only; no generator, independent review, classroom evidence or published resource is claimed.

## GE04 — Test whether lines are parallel / 判別兩直線是否平行

**Mapped objectives:** JS-19-3, JS-19-2 (all Foundation in the retained ledger).

**Demand:** Use angle data to establish or reject parallelism; distinguish a condition from a property with parallelism already given.

**Parameters and guards:** Specified corresponding/alternate-interior/same-side-interior pair under one transversal. Generate satisfying and non-satisfying cases with valid angles 20…160°. Add a vertically opposite-pair insufficient-information contrast, explicitly labelling its different relation.

**Answer contract:** Stable Yes/No/insufficient choice as appropriate plus manual condition/reason. In the named same-side pair the example has a definite Yes, not insufficient.

**Engine readiness:** Choice/reason machinery exists; condition models and nonparallel geometry must be implemented.

**Visual and text alternative:** No parallel arrows before conclusion. Draw from chosen line orientations; angle numbers and marked sectors agree. Text specifies which two angles are paired.

**Sample prompt (EN):** A transversal forms interior angles on the same side of 112° and 68°. Must the two lines be parallel? Explain.

**例題（繁體中文）：** 一條截線形成的一對同旁內角為 112° 和 68°。這兩條直線是否必定平行？請說明。

**Model response:** yes

**Solution (EN):** Yes. 112° + 68° = 180°, so the same-side interior angles are supplementary; this is a condition for the two lines to be parallel.

**解說：** 是。112° + 68° = 180°，即同旁內角互補，符合判別兩直線平行的條件。

**Hint (EN):** Check the sum and the named angle relationship.

**提示：** 檢查角度和，以及題目指定的角對關係。

**Counterexamples and feedback checks:**

- The near-miss 112° and 67° gives No for that same named pair.
- Equality of vertically opposite angles at one intersection alone is insufficient to conclude the two target lines are parallel.

**Paper target:** 3 marks, 5 working lines, about 4 minutes. Calculator not required (local pilot choice). Automatic result marks and manually judged reasoning/method marks must be displayed separately.

**Review state:** design draft; sample self-check only; no generator, independent review, classroom evidence or published resource is claimed.

## GE05 — Triangle interior and exterior angle deductions / 三角形內角與外角的推算

**Mapped objectives:** JS-19-5 (all Foundation in the retained ledger).

**Demand:** Select triangle-sum or exterior-angle reasoning from the requested sector, and name valid reasons.

**Parameters and guards:** Positive whole-degree A,B,C with sum 180° and each 20…140°; include acute and obtuse triangles. Exterior angle uses the actual extended side and equals the two remote interior angles; label the requested sector explicitly.

**Answer contract:** Exact degree value plus separately reviewed reason; interior/exterior variants share one family.

**Engine readiness:** Numeric/reason machinery exists; triangle coordinates and side extension must be generated from the same exact angle data.

**Visual and text alternative:** Construct triangle via angles/intersections; do not draw an arbitrary triangle with inconsistent labels. Accessible text supplies labelled vertices, extension and all givens.

**Sample prompt (EN):** In triangle ABC, angle A = 47° and angle B = 68°. Find angle C and give a reason.

**例題（繁體中文）：** 在三角形 ABC 中，∠A = 47°、∠B = 68°。求 ∠C，並說明理由。

**Model response:** 65

**Solution (EN):** C = 180° − 47° − 68° = 65°, since the interior angles of a triangle sum to 180°. The exterior angle adjacent to C would be 115°.

**解說：** 三角形內角和為 180°，所以 ∠C = 180° − 47° − 68° = 65°。與 ∠C 相鄰的外角則為 115°。

**Hint (EN):** Distinguish the requested interior angle from its adjacent exterior angle.

**提示：** 先分清題目求的是內角，還是相鄰外角。

**Counterexamples and feedback checks:**

- Reject 115° when C is the interior angle; reserve that value for the adjacent exterior sector.
- Reject generated degenerate triangles before display.

**Paper target:** 2 marks, 4 working lines, about 3 minutes. Calculator not required (local pilot choice). Automatic result marks and manually judged reasoning/method marks must be displayed separately.

**Review state:** design draft; sample self-check only; no generator, independent review, classroom evidence or published resource is claimed.

## GE06 — Explain why triangle angle rules hold / 解釋三角形角度性質的由來

**Mapped objectives:** JS-19-5, JS-19-4, JS-19-1 (all Foundation in the retained ledger).

**Demand:** Build a short deductive chain from parallel-line and straight-angle facts, then derive the exterior-angle rule.

**Parameters and guards:** Nondegenerate triangle ABC; line through A parallel BC is a stated construction. Start with a labelled static figure; optional rotation changes appearance only, not proof. No free-proof auto-grading.

**Answer contract:** Open written or structured-statement proof; manual/self rubric. Completing/revealing the model is study, not independent proof evidence.

**Engine readiness:** Requires a multi-statement/manual task layout and rubric; the existing single numeric answer is insufficient.

**Visual and text alternative:** Construct parallel line through A, extend BC at C for the exterior claim, label all relevant rays. Text names construction and which alternate angles correspond.

**Sample prompt (EN):** Draw a line through A parallel to BC in triangle ABC. Use this construction to explain A + B + C = 180°. Then explain why the exterior angle at C equals A + B.

**例題（繁體中文）：** 在三角形 ABC 中，過 A 作 BC 的平行線。利用此輔助線解釋 ∠A + ∠B + ∠C = 180°，再解釋 C 點的外角為何等於 ∠A + ∠B。

**Model response:** manual proof

**Solution (EN):** The angles made by AB and AC with the parallel line equal B and C by alternate interior angles. Together with A they form a straight angle, so A+B+C=180°. If E is the exterior angle adjacent to C, E+C=180°, hence E=A+B.

**解說：** 由平行線的內錯角相等，AB、AC 與輔助平行線所成的角分別等於 ∠B、∠C。它們與 ∠A 拼成一平角，所以 ∠A+∠B+∠C=180°。設 C 點相鄰外角為 E，則 E+∠C=180°，因此 E=∠A+∠B。

**Hint (EN):** Identify the alternate angles at A before using the straight line.

**提示：** 先找出 A 點與 ∠B、∠C 相等的內錯角，再利用平角。

**Counterexamples and feedback checks:**

- Do not use the triangle-sum theorem itself as the starting reason for its proof.
- A measured drawing or a number-only answer does not establish the general statement.

**Paper target:** 4 marks, 6 working lines, about 6 minutes. Calculator not required (local pilot choice). Automatic result marks and manually judged reasoning/method marks must be displayed separately.

**Review state:** design draft; sample self-check only; no generator, independent review, classroom evidence or published resource is claimed.

## Closing connection

Name the angles, list what is given, choose a justified rule and state a reason for every new relationship.

先辨認角、列出已知條件，再選用有根據的性質，為每個新關係說明理由。

Use these deductions in polygons and triangle congruence/similarity after their own scope review.
