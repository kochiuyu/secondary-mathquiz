Every task asks for an exact final number/fraction, without `%` or a currency unit. There is no rounding or tolerance. Original values are positive; changes include zero, −100% and increases above 100%. Recovery excludes −100%, keeping the multiplier nonzero. Retail costs are positive; discounts are below 100%. Interest uses two or three complete years, with no intermediate rounding, fees, deposits or withdrawals. Component original amounts are positive. Tax cases include incomes below allowance, exactly at allowance, exactly at the first band limit and above it. The schedule is explicitly fictional, dated 2026-11-22, fully supplied in every question, and not current Hong Kong law. No inverse salary-from-tax calculation is included.

Four bilingual lessons provide readiness, motivation, contrasting worked examples, a roadmap, takeaways and separate guided practice/check controls. Eight automatically checked final values remain separate from all eight manually reviewed explanations. An edited answer/reason clears self-review. Bounded tax exhaustion is labelled prior exposure. Generated and fixed examples are recorded; this is retained-model freshness, not a claim of universal semantic novelty.
